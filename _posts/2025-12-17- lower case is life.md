lowercase is life.

less is more.
a recent behavioral adaption.

by eliminating the misuse of the uppercase, we reexamine the intent of each choice. uppercase being an intentful perference.
it invites a philosophy of attentiveness. mulling over every detail. finding meaning and value in the smallest of the things. observing the entire microcosm of wonder and detail sitting under our noses in the smallest things in our lives.

im finding myself making every digital organizational component i have the pleasure of fixing my eyes upon into undercase.
changing all folder and file names on my computer undercase
all my youtube and spotify playlists undercase

its like when tattooed individuals go full blackout on their tats, but less dramatic.
transforming the aesthetic of things completely. simplifying. wiping the slate clean.
