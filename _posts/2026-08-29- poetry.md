poetry is letting words fly
giving new abstract meaning to words that have predefined words solely on the basis of how you perceive the way a word leaves your mouth

giving new meaning to old words, in an act of verbal scatting
in the off chance the way these words combine and tickle the brain. associating words that are often not associated
