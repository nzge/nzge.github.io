# nathan-os job-search

**nathan-os job-search** is a personal, single-user tool that I (Nathan Ge) run on my own computer to
organize my job search. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it is not offered to
anyone else.

## What it does

The tool uses my own Google accounts, and only my own accounts, through the Google Workspace APIs:

- **Gmail:** reads job-related email (recruiter messages, job-alert emails) and creates **draft** replies
  for me to review. It never sends an email by itself.
- **Google Sheets:** keeps a spreadsheet of the applications I have submitted and a daily run log.
- **Google Drive, Calendar, and Docs:** looks up and organizes my own job-search files and interview
  events.

I review every draft and every application myself before anything is sent or submitted.
